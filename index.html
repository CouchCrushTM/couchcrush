Why you'll crush on it

Four details that make the couch complete.

We obsessed over the little things so your evenings feel effortless — from the first sip to the last episode.

01

Heated pad

A round glass-top warming pad with a soft amber glow ring keeps your bowl at a cozy 104°F. One-touch controls and auto shut-off after 2 hours.

02

Cup holders & storage

Deep recessed holders for your mug and a tall glass, plus a snack tray, a phone & remote slot, a book groove and a steel valet tray.

03

Expandable legs

Black steel legs with chrome telescoping inners lock at five heights from 9″ to 14″ — low for the couch, tall for the ottoman or bed. Slide, click, done.

04

Solid wood

Cut from FSC-certified American walnut, hand-oiled and finished to be water-resistant, with a brass nameplate on the front. Gets better with every year.

Made for slow evenings

Dinner, a book and the remote, all within reach. Couch Crush sits over your lap without touching it, so warm food stays warm, drinks stay upright and everything else has its own place.

Good questions

Before you get comfortable.

The first batch of 500 units ships this winter. Waitlist members hear first and get a ship date by email before the public launch.

Through a USB-C port on the tray. Plug it into any standard USB-C wall adapter. There are no batteries to charge.

The pad tops out at 104°F, about the warmth of a hot bath, and shuts itself off after 2 hours. The element is sealed beneath the glass top.

The tray is 24″ × 13″ and the legs lock at five heights between 9″ and 14″, so it works over most laps, sofas and ottomans. If you're unsure, measure from your cushion to where you want the tray to sit.

Wipe the glass pad and cup holders with a damp cloth. Once or twice a year, rub in a food-safe oil to keep the walnut finish water-resistant.
